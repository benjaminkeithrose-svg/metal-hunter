# Changelog

Every app release gets an entry here: version, date, and what changed. The newest version is first.
The version at the top must match `APP_VERSION` in `index.html` and the version in `help.html` (`scripts/validate.py` checks this).
Tour and band data updates (the scheduled refreshes) are not versions and are not listed here.

Version numbers: small fixes go 1.0 → 1.0.1, new features go 1.0 → 1.1, the store app will be 2.0.

## v1.0 · 24 Oct 2026
First numbered release.
- Help section (More → Help) covering every screen, label and setting, plus a "What's new" page.
- A one-time "What's new" note after each update.
- Version number shown under More.
- Full project documentation (`docs/DOCUMENTATION.md`) and this changelog.

## v0.9 · 24 Oct 2026
- Share button on every tour, with link previews that show the tour's band, dates and cities.
- Shared links open the tour pinned under "Shared with you".
- "Just added" strip for tours added in the last 7 days.
- Branded preview image for links to the site.

## v0.8 · 23 Oct 2026
- Underground layer: 80 up-and-coming Australian bands, "scene" tours and underground festivals.
- "Include underground and up-and-coming Aussie bands" option in My Metal (off by default).
- "Underground" label on cards and a "Rising Aussie bands" filter on Bands.

## v0.7 · 23 Oct 2026
- Metal Hunter Pro launch sign-up (Shows, More and My Metal) and a privacy page.

## v0.6 · 23 Oct 2026
- Redesign around My Metal: welcome screen, genre groups, states, followed bands.
- Bottom tabs (Shows, Calendar, Presales, Bands, More) and a slim top bar.
- One card per tour with "+ N more dates", "Tickets" buttons, calendar files for any phone.
- Phone-sized calendar, presales grouped by tour, "Rumoured" section.

## v0.5 · 23 Oct 2026
- Punk: 155 bands in 8 groups, including Celtic and Irish punk, and a Punk filter.
- Scheduled news checks (Mon and Thu) and a monthly sweep of every band.

## v0.4 · 22 Oct 2026
- Australian bands' national tours, festivals and cruises, with an Australian filter.
- Band list grown to 1,055, with search and a status filter on Bands.

## v0.3 · 22 Oct 2026
- Installable on iPhone and Android, works offline, and a ⟳ button to check for updates.

## v0.2 · 22 Oct 2026
- Band tracker grown to 547 bands; Info tab for scope notes and labels.

## v0.1 · 22 Oct 2026
- First version: calendar of international heavy tours in Australia with ticket links and on-sale times.
- Two looks: Runestone and Xerox Grind.

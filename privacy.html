Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026.

Pro launch sign-up

If you sign up to hear when Metal Hunter Pro launches, we keep:

  • your email address
  • the genres and states you picked in My Metal, if any
  • which screen you signed up from, and the date

We use it to email you about the Pro launch and to see which genres and states want alerts most. We don't use it for anything else, and we don't sell it or share it with anyone.

Where it's kept

In a database run by Supabase, on servers in Sydney. Only the Metal Hunter team can read it.

Unsubscribe or delete

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. To have your details deleted, reply to any email from us and ask, and we'll remove them.

Everything else

Your My Metal settings, followed bands and chosen look are saved on your own device, not sent to us. Ticket links take you to the official seller, whose own privacy policy applies there.

We may count visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites.

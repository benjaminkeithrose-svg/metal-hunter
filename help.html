Getting started

Metal Hunter lists every metal, hardcore and punk tour coming to Australia: international acts at any venue size, Australian bands' national tours and festivals, and (if you want them) underground and up-and-coming Aussie bands. Every show links to the official ticket seller and says when tickets open.

The first time you open it, pick your genres and states. That's My Metal. You can skip it and see everything.

The tabs along the bottom:

Shows
Upcoming tours, soonest first, one card per tour.
Calendar
A month view. Dots mark days with shows; tap a day to see them.
Presales
When tickets go on sale, in Sydney time.

Every band we track. Tap ☆ to follow one.

My Metal

My Metal is what you're into. Tap ⚙ Edit on Shows (or More → Edit My Metal) to change it.

  • Genres: pick any of the nine groups, from Heavy, thrash and power to Folk, pagan and symphonic. Pick none to see every genre.
  • Where: pick your states. Pick none for all of Australia.
  • Bands you follow: their tours always show, whatever genres you picked.
  • Include Aussie bands' tours: on by default.
  • Include underground and up-and-coming Aussie bands: off by default. See Underground.
  • All-ages shows only: hides 18+ and 16+ shows, and shows whose age rule we don't know yet. This applies on All shows too.
  • Hide sold-out shows, and Show every date separately (one card per date instead of one per tour).

Switch between My Metal and All shows at the top of Shows, Calendar and Presales. Your settings are saved on your phone.

Reading a show card

Each card shows the next date in your states, the venue, the support acts and the sale details. Tap + N more dates for the rest of the tour, and i for notes and where the information came from.

Following bands

On the Bands tab, search for a band and tap ☆ to follow it (★ means you follow it). You can also add bands in My Metal. Followed bands' tours always show in My Metal, even outside your chosen genres or the underground setting.

Tap a band's name to see its latest news: touring overseas, rumoured, or nothing announced yet. Use the filter to see just the bands you follow, bands on the calendar, rumoured tours, or rising Aussie bands.

Underground and up-and-coming bands

Switch on Include underground and up-and-coming Aussie bands in My Metal to see smaller Australian bands' tours, launch tours and underground festivals: bands not yet headlining rooms bigger than about 500 people. It's off by default.

We don't list one-off pub gigs, only tours and festivals.

Calendar

Use the arrows to change month. Days with shows have dots (up to three); tap a day to see its shows underneath. The calendar follows the My Metal / All shows switch.

Presales

Tickets opening soon lists presales and general sales coming up, grouped by tour, with the time in Sydney time. Opened in the last two weeks shows sales that have just opened. Presale codes usually come from the promoter, the venue or a card company; sign up with them to get one.

Sharing a tour

Tap Share on any card. On a phone, your share menu opens (Messenger, WhatsApp, text and so on); on a computer, the link is copied. The link shows the tour's band, dates and cities when you paste it. When your mate opens it, the tour sits at the top under Shared with you.

Install it on your phone

  • iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open the site in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

It then opens like an app and works offline with the last shows it loaded.

Updates

New tours and presales are added twice a week, and every band is re-checked each month. The app updates itself when you open it. Tap ⟳ in the top corner to check straight away. After an app update you'll see a short "What's new" note once.

The version you're on is shown at the bottom of More.

Metal Hunter Pro

Pro is coming: push alerts when a tour in your genres hits your state, and reminders before presales open. Leave your email on the Pro card (end of Shows, or under More) and we'll tell you when it launches. Metal Hunter itself stays free.

Privacy

Your My Metal settings, followed bands and look are stored on your phone, not sent to us. If you sign up for the Pro launch, we keep your email and chosen genres and states. Read the privacy page for details.

FAQ

A tour I know about isn't listed. Why?

It may have been announced since the last update (twice a week), it may be a one-off pub gig, or it may be a New Zealand date, which we don't list. Underground tours only show if you've switched them on.

Why can't I see a tour my mate can?

Your My Metal settings are probably filtering it out. Switch to All shows at the top of Shows.

Is the information official?

It comes from promoters, official ticket sellers and music news. Ticket links always go to the official seller. Check the seller before you buy, especially for anything marked Unconfirmed.

Can I go if I'm under 18?

Look for the age label on the card, or switch on All-ages shows only in My Metal. If there's no label, check the ticket page: venues set the rule show by show.

Do you sell tickets?

No. We link you to the official seller.

Is pop-punk included?

No. Punk is in (Celtic and Irish, street punk and Oi!, '77, skate, horror punk and more), but pop-punk isn't.

Can I suggest a tour or a band?

Yes. Tell us where you found Metal Hunter, or reply to any email from us.
